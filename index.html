    Initial release.

    What's new

    • Six-band mastering EQ built on the Sonic Studio Mastering EQ filter engine
    • 15 filter types per band: Parametric, Band Pass, Band Stop, Notch, Hi Shelf, Lo Shelf, Hi Pass, Lo Pass, DC Block, Emphasis, De-emphasis, De-emph/DC, RIAA and De-RIAA (or None)
    • Order 1–4, with passband ripple and stopband attenuation for the high-order filters; each band shows only the controls its type uses
    • Per-band On/Off and a global EQ ON switch
    • Visualizer with per-band curves, a dotted summed curve and draggable band nodes; 24 / 12 / 6 / 3 dB scale
    • Output gain fader (±12 dB)
    • Dither: Bypass, Round, Dither (TPDF) and Noise Shape, at 16, 20 or 24 bits, with silence gate and noise-shaping band
    • Peak and RMS meters for every channel, with held readouts, Peak Hold and Meter Release settings
    • A–D compare slots, saved with the session
    • Mono through 16 channels, including 7.1.4 and 9.1.6
    • AU and VST3, licensed with soundBlade 3.0 (included with soundBlade); or AU, VST3 and AAX with a Sonic Studio Mastering EQ iLok licence
    • macOS ARM64 + Intel universal
    • Installer lets you choose which formats to install (Customize); an uninstaller is included on the disk image
